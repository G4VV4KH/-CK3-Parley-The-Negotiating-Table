# Parley 1.2.0 — Currency trading rules

- Choose when prestige and piety can be traded. Both still default to **Available**.
- Prestige can be limited to rulers of the same faith, or transferred strictly from a higher level of fame to a lower one. The existing equal-or-higher-fame option remains available.
- Piety can be limited to the same faith, different faiths, or the same broader religion. A further option permits same-faith transfers only from a strictly higher level of devotion to a lower one.
- Permissions follow the actual giver and receiver. Unavailable currency rows disappear from the relevant side of the negotiating table.
- Auto-balance, AI pricing and final agreement validation obey the same currency restrictions.
- Compact rule labels and updated descriptions are included across all nine supported languages.
- **Getting started** now includes a detailed guide to all six public game rules, their defaults and useful combinations. Steam links to the full guide from its shorter introduction.

Targets CK3 **1.20.0.3**. AGOT support remains on hold pending its CK3 1.20 update. No new required mod has been added.
